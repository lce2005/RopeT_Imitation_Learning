# Sources and contribution scope

This is Team 3's course-project work for Deep Learning, Fall 2026, Sookmyung Women's University, taught by Prof. Joo Yong Sim.

- Course starter repository: https://github.com/jooyongsim/deeplearning_SMWU_2026 (assignment directory: `DL_assignment01/`).
- The original assignment attributes `sim/rope_env.py`, `robot_env.py`, `success.py`, `level_table.py` and `data/` to `ropeT_sim_bc` in https://github.com/jooyongsim/real2sim_deformable.
- M3 work: feature construction, temporal sample construction, normalization, MLP policy and agent, BC training, and the PPO fine-tuning experiment. These files retain instructor-provided interfaces and helper code; they are not entirely original files.
- Simulator, teleoperation, checking, evaluation and utility code are based on the provided assignment framework. Demonstration collection and final simulator evaluation are team work.

The inspected local course checkout contains no LICENSE or COPYING file. No new license is assigned to third-party code or assets here. Attribution alone does not establish permission to redistribute. Confirm course policy, instructor permission and team agreement before public release. Keep this preparation private pending that confirmation; check any course restrictions on private third-party hosting as well.
